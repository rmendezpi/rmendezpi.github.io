About Me

I am a Research Fellow in Epidemiology and Statistics at UCL Great Ormond Street Institute of Child Health, working in Population Health Improvement UK and the NIHR Children and Families Policy Research Unit. I use national linked administrative data, ECHILD, to understand inequalities in young people’s access to mental health services.

I completed my PhD at the UCL Social Research Institute in 2026. It evaluated Multi-Agency Safeguarding Hubs and other multi-agency front door services in children’s social care across England. I was research lead on the Evaluation of Multi-Agency Safeguarding Hubs, commissioned by the Department for Education. It contributed evidence to the Children’s Wellbeing and Schools Act 2026 and was cited in the Sara Sharif Child Safeguarding Practice Review.

I have taught quantitative methods at UCL since 2022 and teach on the Introduction to ECHILD course for researchers using linked data. Before moving into child health and social care, I worked on the measurement of job quality in Latin America and the EU.

Research Interests

  • Inequalities in referral and access to NHS mental health services for children and young people
  • Intersectional inequalities, using multilevel analysis of individual heterogeneity and discriminatory accuracy (MAIHDA)
  • Linked administrative data across health, education and social care (ECHILD)
  • Children’s services and child protection, including multi-agency safeguarding hubs
  • Quasi-experimental methods: difference-in-differences, synthetic controls, interrupted time series
