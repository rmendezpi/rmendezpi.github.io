Professional Summary

Quantitative social scientist using national linked administrative data to understand inequalities in children’s and young people’s mental health and access to mental health services. Combines multilevel and quasi-experimental methods with qualitative work, evidence reviews and co-production with young people and parents. Background in the evaluation of children’s services and social policy.
